Clear Thinking About Content, Tools, and Careers

January 9, 2026

I’ve spent most of my career working at the intersection of content, systems, and people.

That has meant building and leading documentation teams, working on complex technical products, and thinking a lot about how information actually gets used—not just how it’s written. Over time, I’ve come to believe that good content isn’t just about clarity on the page. It’s about reducing friction, creating confidence, and helping people move forward more quickly.

This blog is a place to explore those ideas.

I’m interested in how content functions as infrastructure: how documentation supports scale, how tools (including AI) change the way we create and maintain information, and how small decisions compound over time. I’m also interested in careers—especially the less linear ones—and how we position our work as roles, technologies, and expectations continue to shift.

You won’t find hot takes or perfectly polished frameworks here. What you will find is thinking in progress: lessons learned from building systems, revisiting assumptions, and occasionally realizing that something I once felt confident about needs to be reexamined.

Some posts will be practical. Others will be more reflective. Many will sit somewhere in between.

If you work in documentation, content strategy, developer experience, or any role where clarity and systems matter, my hope is that something in this space is useful—or at least helps you think about your own work a little differently.

Thanks for reading.
